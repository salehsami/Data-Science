Method and scope

A zone is covered when (site_x − zone_x)² + (site_y − zone_y)² ≤ radius² for at least one selected site. The objective sums each covered zone's priority population once. The exact search evaluates all subsets with up to four sites and rejects those costing more than €1,000.

This fictional pilot assumes every candidate is eligible. It does not model buildings, terrain, sensor reliability, maintenance costs or temperature representativeness. A planning radius is not a physical sensing range.
