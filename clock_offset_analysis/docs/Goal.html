As a data scientist supporting a cold-storage operations team, I will investigate disagreement between two colocated temperature sensors and assess whether a consistent clock offset explains their mismatched readings.

In DBeaver, I will inspect synthetic sensor readings for duplicate timestamps, missing samples, sampling intervals and overlapping coverage. In Firefox, I will use a prepared local analysis dashboard to compare the sensor series at their original timestamps, then evaluate candidate clock offsets from minus ten to plus ten minutes in thirty-second increments.

I will estimate the offset using the first three hours of readings, examining correlation, temperature differences and ambiguity between candidate offsets. I will then apply the selected offset unchanged to a separate final hour and assess whether agreement improves.

In Visual Studio Code, I will save verification queries, inspect exported aligned readings and complete a diagnostic memo recommending whether a timestamp correction is supported, requires further investigation or should be rejected.
